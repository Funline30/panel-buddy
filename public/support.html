⚡ AmpMap Help and Support

AmpMap maps your breaker panel, outlets, lights and circuit loads, with QR stickers and load checks. Free, no ads, on the web and on iPhone.

Contact us

Email margoittac@gmail.com. Tell us what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened (a screenshot helps). We usually answer within 1 to 2 business days.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. Tap Try the demo to use AmpMap with a sample home; demo data stays on your device. Create a free account to sync across your phone and computer.
  • Forgot your password? On the sign-in screen choose Reset my password and we email you a link.
  • Scan the panel label needs internet the first time (it downloads the free reading engine). Handwritten labels may only read partly; you can fix every line on the review screen before saving.
  • QR stickers link to your panel. Turn on a share link for a home if you want anyone with the sticker to see a read-only directory.
  • Let an electrician edit: tap Who can edit on your home and make an edit code. Turn it off any time.
  • Printing stickers: on iPhone, Print opens the iOS print sheet; choose your printer, or Save to Files for a PDF.

Delete your account

In the app: More → Delete my account, enter your password and type DELETE. This permanently deletes your account and all of its data. Or email us from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.

Safety

AmpMap is an organizing tool, not a substitute for a licensed electrician. Load numbers are estimates. Always turn the breaker off and verify with a tester before touching wiring.
